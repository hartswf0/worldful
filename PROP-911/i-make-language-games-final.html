What is a prompt?

Calling a sentence a “prompt” explains almost nothing. Its force comes from the game in which it is played.

BOUNDARY31
NOT
a taxonomy of prompts — the twelve are games the prompt is played in

a theory of the model — claims about encounters, not weights

“just semantics” — a grammar-constrained decoder cannot emit outside its grammar; injection exists because instruction and data are one material

a benchmark · a history of direct influence
HOW IT WOULD BE WRONG
1  a session crosses a drift edge without its stated trigger → the switchyard is redrawn

2  a description loses a distinction and the required response does not change → thick prompting is over-thick there

3  independent readers cannot say which game a staged encounter starts in → the frame is not shared

4  the world does not answer back with evidence → it was not a worldtext
Define by negation, then hand over the knife. Each falsifier kills one concept; the last line is the boundary of the claim.
