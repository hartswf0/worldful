## PART I: The McEnerney Disciplinary Value & Instability Transformation (Prompt 042)

### 1. The Tutino Audit (Curing the "Horizontal Thinking Dump")
* **The Vulnerability in the Current Draft:** 
  A conventional reading might treat *Deep Play at the Aperture* horizontally: *"Here are twelve language games from Wittgenstein, here are six prototypes I built at elsewhere, and here is what each prototype does."* 
  An outside reviewer or unsympathetic committee member asks: *"Who cares about your six idiosyncratic prototypes? Why should AI engineers care about Geertz, and why should humanists care about LDraw syntax?"*
* **The Vertical Re-Orientation:** 
  The paper is not a report on six software projects. It is an **epistemic intervention into the foundational breakdown of generative interaction**. The six prototypes are external resistance chambers where language was forced to collide with reality.

---

### 2. The Dual Disciplinary Instability

#### A. The Engineering / Computer Science Instability:
* **Prevailing Consensus:** The prompt is an input string entering a neutral textual aperture ($R = f(u, c)$). Alignment, instruction tuning, and prompt engineering can optimize the model toward reliable behavior within this single channel.
* **The Fatal Instability:** **The Aperture Error.** Compressing heterogeneous speech practices into one text box forces systems to infer authority from imperative syntax alone. When data resembles command, prompt injection occurs. When generation impersonates retrieval, citations hallucinate. When state is not externalized, stories drift. **These are not alignment bugs; they are architectural category errors caused by hiding the language game.**

#### B. The Humanities / Critical AI Studies Instability:
* **Prevailing Consensus:** Humanities scholars critique generative AI at the level of discourse, ethics, or post-hoc ideology critique—treating "executable language" as a rhetorical metaphor or examining output transcripts.
* **The Fatal Instability:** **Discursive Impotence.** Once language directly triggers computational state transitions, robots, and legal exposures, critique at the level of the transcript is blind to where power actually operates. **Humanistic distinctions (Austin’s felicity, Geertz’s deep play, Sacks’ turn-taking) must become inspectable architectural requirements, or humanists forfeit any say in the operational infrastructure of society.**

### 4. The Re-Engineered "Vertical Value" Opening Threshold
*(Moving the End to the Front: Replacing throat-clearing with immediate disciplinary rupture)*

> A language model is handed three words: *“Draw a circle.”* Contemporary computer science treats this as an input string conditioned on context: $R = f(u, c)$. This is the foundational error of generative design. 
> 
> Nothing in the surface string tells us whether the speaker is an architect issuing a command, a researcher probing a model’s boundary, an artist writing an open score, or a client making a commission. By collapsing all human linguistic practices into a single rectangular text aperture, generative systems force probabilistic decoders to guess the social authority, evidentiary standards, and execution rights of an utterance from syntax alone. The result is not merely bad prose; it is prompt injection, hallucinated evidence, and catastrophic state drift. 
> 
> The solution is neither more reinforcement learning nor larger system prompts. The solution is an **Operative Humanities**: an engineering and humanistic methodology that recovers the operative game ($R = f(u, c, g)$) by inserting inspectable computational machinery—validators, explicit state engines, diff viewports, and trace logs—into the gap between words and convincing results.
