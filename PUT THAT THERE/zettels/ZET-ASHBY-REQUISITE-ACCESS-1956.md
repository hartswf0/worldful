ZET-ASHBY-REQUISITE-ACCESS-1956

TITLE:
Requisite Variety Must Reach the Regulator Through an Effective Channel

SOURCE:
W. Ross Ashby - An Introduction to Cybernetics - 1956. ([Ashby Info](https://ashby.info/Ashby-Introduction-to-Cybernetics.pdf))

PASSAGE:
Ashby states that regulatory capacity is limited by the regulator’s capacity as a communication channel.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
A temporal-access qualification to the proposed Law of Requisite Description.

LOCAL MOVE:
Corrects the weaker claim that requisite distinctions merely need to “exist somewhere” in the coupled system. They must become available to the regulating operation in usable form.

SOURCE TERMS:
requisite variety; disturbance; regulator; channel of communication; outcome; correction channel.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A repository, screenshot, prior message, or tool schema can contain the needed distinction yet fail to regulate behavior if the agent cannot retrieve it when deciding.

QUESTION:
When does environmental information count as operative context rather than merely available information?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Is prompt thickness better defined by accessible requisite distinction at decision time than by total contextual information?

MECHANISM:
A disturbance can be countered only if distinctions sufficient to select the relevant regulatory response pass through an effective channel.

FORMAL SHIFT:
DESCRIPTION EXISTS → DESCRIPTION IS ACCESSIBLE BEFORE ACTION.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
Ashby relates regulatory variety to disturbance variety and communication-channel capacity.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION - NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
For each action-relevant disturbance class d, the discriminating information must be recoverable by the controller before the response deadline.

TENSION:
Ashby’s mathematical variety is not identical to semantic “description thickness.”

MISSING:
A principled mapping from contextual distinctions to measurable information variety in agentic interfaces.

BOUNDARY:
“Law of Requisite Description” remains our extension, not Ashby’s term.

CITATION TRAIL:
Ashby 1956 → requisite variety → proposed requisite description → progressive context disclosure.

TEST:
Keep total stored context fixed while varying whether the relevant detail is directly visible, retrievable by tool call, or inaccessible until after action; compare regulation success.

PLATFORM:
Cybernetics / agent context.
