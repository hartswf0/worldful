TITLE:
Before “Direction of Fit,” Anscombe’s Shopping Lists Locate Where the Mistake Lies

SOURCE:
G. E. M. Anscombe - Intention - §32; text reproduced by Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy. ([Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/sum2016/entries/anscombe/)) ([Canonical Link](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/anscombe/))

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE]
"Let us consider a man going round a town with a shopping list in his hand. Now it is clear that the relation of this list to the things he buys and of his list to what he buys is different from the relation of a list which a detective following him might make of what he buys. If the list and the things that the man buys do not agree, and if this and this alone constitutes a mistake, then the mistake is not in the list but in the man's performance... whereas if the detective's record and what the man bought do not agree, then the mistake is in the record." — G. E. M. Anscombe, Intention, §32, pp. 56–57 (1957)

RESEARCH OBJECT:
A genealogy correction for direction-of-fit claims in generative critique.

LOCAL MOVE:
Replaces the over-clean attribution “Anscombe’s direction of fit” with her actual distinction between kinds of error relation.

SOURCE TERMS:
shopping list; order; intention; record; mistake; performance.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The same propositional contents can stand in distinct practical relations to what occurs without Anscombe needing the later phrase “direction of fit.”

QUESTION:
Does “That’s not a circle” locate a mistake in the artifact, the description, the interpreter, or the shared criterion?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can agentic criticism be classified by where the interaction assigns responsibility for mismatch?

MECHANISM:
The practical role of a representation determines which side is revised when representation and event diverge.

FORMAL SHIFT:
DIRECTION OF FIT AS SLOGAN → LOCATION OF CORRECTABLE ERROR IN A PRACTICE.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
Anscombe’s list/order/record comparison.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION - NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
Mismatch(prompt, artifact) + language-game → revise artifact | revise description | revise interpretation | revise criterion.

TENSION:
Later speech-act theory generalizes Anscombe into direction-of-fit terminology that can obscure her action-theoretic context. ([Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/sum2010/entries/speech-acts/))

MISSING:
Primary genealogy from Anscombe §32 through Searle’s formal “direction of fit” terminology.

BOUNDARY:
Do not state that Anscombe coined “direction of fit.”

Anscombe 1957 → later speech-act theory → criticism-as-programming.
