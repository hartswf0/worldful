TITLE:
Making Intent Machine-Legible Can Turn Description Into Discipline

SOURCE:
Lucy Suchman - “Do Categories Have Politics? The Language/Action Perspective Reconsidered” - 1994. ([Lancaster University](https://www.lancaster.ac.uk/fass/doc_library/sociology/Suchman_categories_1994.pdf))

PASSAGE:
Suchman characterizes The Coordinator as technological “intention-accounting” and questions the disciplinary consequences of categorizing speakers’ intent.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The politics of turning language-games into computable categories.

LOCAL MOVE:
Adds a political failure mode to thick prompting: increasing machine legibility may force situated human action into the system’s preferred ontology.

SOURCE TERMS:
categorization; intent; discipline; control; coordination technologies; intention-accounting.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
A representation designed to support coordination can reshape the coordination by requiring participants to inhabit its categories.

QUESTION:
When does an operative description support a human practice, and when does it prescribe the practice it claims merely to represent?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does LLM-based ambiguity tolerance genuinely reduce categorical discipline, or merely hide classification inside inaccessible inference?

MECHANISM:
A system makes selected action categories obligatory or consequential; participants adapt their conduct to those categories.

FORMAL SHIFT:
MODEL OF COMMUNICATION → INTERFACE CATEGORY → MODEL FOR COMMUNICATION.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
Suchman critiques speech-act categories as political features of CSCW design.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION - NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
CATEGORY becomes operative when access to next actions depends on passing through it.

TENSION:
Removing explicit categories can decrease visible constraint while increasing opaque model-mediated categorization.

MISSING:
Comparative study of explicit workflow categories versus latent LLM intent classification.

BOUNDARY:
Suchman’s argument is institutional and political, not simply a usability critique.

CITATION TRAIL:
Winograd 1987 → Suchman 1994 → model-of/model-for → human-centered translation.

TEST:
Hold a coordination task constant; compare explicit categorical workflow, free-form deterministic parser, and LLM-mediated interaction; document who can inspect, contest, or revise the operative categories.
