ID:
ZET-MATURANA-ORGANIZATION-STRUCTURE-2002

TITLE:
Identity Persists Through Structural Change Only While Organization Is Conserved

SOURCE:
Humberto Maturana Romesín - “Autopoiesis, Structural Coupling and Cognition” - 2002. ([Reflexus](https://reflexus.org/wp-content/uploads/Autopoiesis-structural-coupling-and-cognition.pdf))

PASSAGE:
Maturana distinguishes changing structure from conserved organization; class identity persists only while organization is conserved.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Transformation invariants and artifact identity.

LOCAL MOVE:
Sharpens the eigenform branch by separating what may vary from what must remain conserved, while explicitly marking application to graphic objects as analogical.

SOURCE TERMS:
organization; structure; class identity; conservation; change of state; disintegration.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Identity can be defined by relations conserved through transformation rather than by preservation of material components.

QUESTION:
What is the “organization” of YELLOW-CIRCLE as distinct from one particular raster or representation?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can prompt-based editing discover an artifact’s organization empirically by perturbing structure until observers withdraw identity judgments?

MECHANISM:
Structural transformations are tolerated while the relations constituting class identity remain conserved.

FORMAL SHIFT:
IDENTITY = SAME REPRESENTATION → IDENTITY = CONSERVED ORGANIZATION ACROSS REPRESENTATIONAL CHANGE.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
Maturana’s organization/structure distinction concerns systems and is centrally developed for living systems.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION - NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
STRUCTURE = concrete pixels/code/trajectory.
ORGANIZATION = relations whose conservation supports classification as YELLOW-CIRCLE.

TENSION:
Graphic objects are not autopoietic systems; transferring this distinction requires justification.

MISSING:
A non-biological account showing when Maturana intended the organization/structure distinction to apply generally.

BOUNDARY:
This zettel does not claim that a rendered circle possesses biological organization.

CITATION TRAIL:
Maturana 2002 → eigenform → permissible transformations → Yellow Circle.

TEST:
Systematically vary size, position, hue, edge quality, topology, and representation; record the boundary at which observers cease preserving CIRCLE and YELLOW-CIRCLE identity.

PLATFORM:
Cybernetics / experimental semantics.
