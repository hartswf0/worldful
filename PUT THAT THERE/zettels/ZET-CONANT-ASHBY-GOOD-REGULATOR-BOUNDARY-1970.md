TITLE:
The Good Regulator Theorem Is Conditional, Not a License to Call Every Successful Prompt a World Model

SOURCE:
Roger C. Conant and W. Ross Ashby - “Every Good Regulator of a System Must Be a Model of That System” - 1970. ([Governance Foundation](https://governance.foundation/assets/frameworks/other/Conant_Ashby%20Every%20Good%20Regulator%20of%20a%20system%20must%20be%20a%20model%20of%20that%20system.pdf))

PASSAGE:
The theorem concerns regulators maximally both successful and simple “under very broad conditions”; the paper notes that exact assumptions are specified.

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The boundary of the model-of-world claim in world-to-world prompting.

LOCAL MOVE:
Prevents overextension of the Good Regulator theorem into a universal claim that every successful agent must explicitly represent the full system it acts upon.

SOURCE TERMS:
regulator; model; isomorphism; maximally successful; simple; exact assumptions.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The memorable slogan is stronger than what may safely be transferred outside the theorem’s formal assumptions.

QUESTION:
Which assumptions of the theorem are satisfied by an LLM acting through a GUI?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can successful world-to-world transformation rely on partial, transient, or action-specific models rather than a globally isomorphic world model?

MECHANISM:
Under the theorem’s specified conditions, optimal regulation imposes a model relation between regulator and regulated system.

FORMAL SHIFT:
EVERY SUCCESSFUL AGENT HAS A WORLD MODEL → CERTAIN OPTIMAL REGULATORS REQUIRE A MODEL RELATION UNDER DEFINED CONDITIONS.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
Conant and Ashby prove a formal theorem with stated assumptions.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION - NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
Treat “model adequacy” as an empirical variable indexed to task and disturbance set rather than a universal property.

TENSION:
The world-to-world framework currently risks using “model” too loosely.

MISSING:
Formal comparison between theorem variables and agentic GUI interaction variables.

BOUNDARY:
Do not cite the Good Regulator theorem as proof that contemporary foundation models contain human-like internal world models.

CITATION TRAIL:
Conant and Ashby 1970 → requisite description → world-to-world model.

TEST:
Map an agent task onto the theorem’s exact variables and assumptions before invoking it; record which assumptions fail.

PLATFORM:
Cybernetics / AI theory.
