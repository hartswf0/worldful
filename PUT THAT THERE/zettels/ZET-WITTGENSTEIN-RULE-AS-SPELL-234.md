ZET-WITTGENSTEIN-RULE-AS-SPELL-234

TITLE:
Correct Rule Following Can Feel Like Being Guided by a Spell

SOURCE:
Ludwig Wittgenstein - Philosophical Investigations - §§232-238. ([Wiley-Blackwell](https://www.blackwellpublishing.com/content/bpl_images/content_store/sample_chapter/9780631231271/Wittgenstein.pdf))

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE]
"When does one have the thought: the possible movements of a machine are already there in it in some mysterious way?... We are inclined to say that the machine in moving is already acting out a predetermined sequence. We feel as if we were being guided by a spell." — Ludwig Wittgenstein, Philosophical Investigations, §234 (1953)

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Prompt superstition as phenomenology of competent rule following.

LOCAL MOVE:
Supplies a direct philosophical precursor to the prompt-superstition branch: successful conformity can coexist with a mistaken phenomenology of how the rule causes the action.

SOURCE TERMS:
rule; inspiration; guided; spell; agreement; calculating; training.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
Feeling strongly guided by a symbolic rule supplies no proof that the rule mechanically contains all of its applications.

QUESTION:
When prompt users report that a phrase “makes the model think correctly,” are they identifying an operative distinction or narrating the phenomenology of successful coordination?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can ritualized prompting train real competence while preserving a false causal account of why the competence works?

MECHANISM:
Reliable practice produces agreement; the participant retrospectively experiences the explicit rule as if it generated the successful continuation.

FORMAL SHIFT:
SUCCESSFUL COORDINATION ≠ VERIFIED CAUSAL THEORY OF THE RULE.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
Wittgenstein contrasts rule-following, inspiration, training, public regularity, and the feeling of magical guidance.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION - NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
PROMPT_RITUAL may be operator-operative even when phrase-level machine causality is unverified.

TENSION:
Wittgenstein is not discussing probabilistic models or superstition in AI use.

MISSING:
Controlled prompt-ablation studies that separate operator effects from model effects.

BOUNDARY:
The analogy concerns rule phenomenology, not a claim that prompting is literally magical thinking.

CITATION TRAIL:
Philosophical Investigations §§232-238 → prompt superstition → operative ritual → prompt ablation.

TEST:
Have users employ a ritual prompt whose model-side components are progressively ablated while preserving the user’s planning ritual; separately measure output and operator performance.

PLATFORM:
Rule-following / human-AI interaction.
