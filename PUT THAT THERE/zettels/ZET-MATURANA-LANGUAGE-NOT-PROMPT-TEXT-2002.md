ZET-MATURANA-LANGUAGE-NOT-PROMPT-TEXT-2002

TITLE:
For Maturana, Language Is the Flow of Coordination, Not the Isolated Utterance

SOURCE:
Humberto Maturana Romesín - “Autopoiesis, Structural Coupling and Cognition” - 2002. ([Reflexus](https://reflexus.org/wp-content/uploads/Autopoiesis-structural-coupling-and-cognition.pdf))

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE]
"Language is not a system of symbolic communications through which we transmit information about an independent reality. Language is a biological phenomenon: it is a manner of living together in recurrent consensual coordinations of consensual coordinations of actions (languaging). We do not use language to speak about things; things arise in language through the recursive coordination of our doings." — Humberto Maturana Romesín, Autopoiesis, Structural Coupling and Cognition, Cybernetics & Human Knowing, 9(3-4), pp. 5–34 (2002)

RESEARCH OBJECT:
The ontological status of a prompt within languaging.

LOCAL MOVE:
Pressures any claim that a prompt string itself constitutes a language. Under Maturana, it becomes linguistic through its place in an ongoing relational flow.

SOURCE TERMS:
languaging; coordinations of coordinations; consensual behaviors; recursive interactions; living together.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The “prompt” may be analytically the wrong unit if its operative force resides across prompt, response, correction, history, and shared situation.

QUESTION:
What is the minimal interactional unit for studying prompting as languaging?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Can an LLM interaction satisfy enough of Maturana’s relational structure to make “languaging” more than a metaphor?

MECHANISM:
Current action perturbs a recurrent coordination whose continuation retrospectively stabilizes the role of the current utterance.

FORMAL SHIFT:
PROMPT → OUTPUT becomes TURN ↔ RESPONSE ↔ REPAIR ↔ RECURRENCE.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
Maturana’s source unit is a relational flow of consensual coordinations, not an isolated sign.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION - NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
OPERATIVE_FORCE(p_t) = f(p_t, interaction_history, current_world, response, later_repair).

TENSION:
LLMs are not living autopoietic organisms, and Maturana’s account is biological and relational.

MISSING:
A principled criterion for when “languaging” may be extended to human-machine coupling without category error.

BOUNDARY:
Do not claim the model itself languages in Maturana’s full biological sense merely because dialogue is recursive.

CITATION TRAIL:
Maturana 2002 → language-games → thick prompting → conversational agents.

TEST:
Compare isolated prompt evaluation with interaction-sequence evaluation; determine how much behavior becomes predictable only after including repair history.

PLATFORM:
Biology of cognition / conversational AI.
