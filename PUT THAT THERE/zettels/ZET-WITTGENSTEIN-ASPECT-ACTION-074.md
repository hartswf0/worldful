ZET-WITTGENSTEIN-ASPECT-ACTION-074

TITLE:
Aspect Seeing Already Changes How an Order Is Carried Out

SOURCE:
Ludwig Wittgenstein - Philosophical Investigations - §74. ([Wiley-Blackwell](https://www.blackwellpublishing.com/content/bpl_images/content_store/sample_chapter/9780631231271/Wittgenstein.pdf))

PASSAGE:
[QUOTE]
"I see a picture of a box which can be seen either as a box or as three triangular planes... According as I see it, I give a different description of it, and also I make a different use of it. Seeing an aspect and imagining are subject to the will." — Ludwig Wittgenstein, Philosophical Investigations, Part II, §74 (1953)

RESEARCH OBJECT:
Aspect perception as a determinant of operative continuation.

LOCAL MOVE:
Strengthens the Duck-Rabbit branch: aspect is not merely alternate labeling; it can change compliance with an instruction.

SOURCE TERMS:
see; sample; use; rules; schematic drawing; order; something like this.

WHAT BECAME STRANGE:
The visual stimulus can remain fixed while the correct next action changes.

QUESTION:
Can aspect instability be measured as divergence in action rather than divergence in verbal classification?

DEEPER QUESTION:
Does recognizing an object amount partly to acquiring a grammar of possible continuations?

MECHANISM:
A way of seeing organizes a sample under a rule of use; that organization determines what counts as an analogous object.

FORMAL SHIFT:
SAME IMAGE → DIFFERENT ASPECT → DIFFERENT VALID ACTION.

SOURCE FORMALISM:
Wittgenstein directly connects seeing a sample differently with using it differently.

OUR FORMALIZATION:
[OUR FORMALIZATION - NOT SOURCE SYNTAX]
ASPECT_INSTABILITY(x) = change in licensed action-space under minimal contextual perturbation while x remains invariant.

TENSION:
Wittgenstein does not offer a computational metric or claim every aspect switch is linguistically caused.

MISSING:
Experimental HCI and vision-language work measuring prompt-induced changes in action around invariant visual stimuli.

BOUNDARY:
Do not reduce seeing-as to classification labels alone.

CITATION TRAIL:
Philosophical Investigations §§73-74 → aspect perception → Yellow Dot / Duck-Rabbit experiment.

TEST:
Hold one yellow dot pixel-identical; prime it as BUTTON, SUN, NODE, TARGET, or PROGRAM; then issue the same or analogous follow-up and compare action selection.

PLATFORM:
Philosophy of perception / multimodal agents.
