Dissertation Contribution Statement
One-Sentence Version
My dissertation contributes
operative description
and
thick prompting
as a theory-method pair for studying
worldtext
: the recursive media environments produced when prompts, outputs, revisions, interfaces, archives, and interpretations interact.
Paragraph Version
This dissertation enters the fields of digital media, HCI, media studies, AI ethics, narrative theory, and practice-based research. It responds to a limit in current discourse: generative AI is often framed as either a tool, an author, a hallucination machine, or a productivity system, but not as a recursive cultural system where description becomes operative. My intervention is to develop the concepts of
operative description
,
worldtext
, and
thick prompting
as a way to study how prompts, outputs, revisions, archives, prototypes, and interpretations form generated worlds. The artifact is a dissertation supported by selected case studies, prompt-output logs, diagrams, and prototype systems. The opened door is a usable framework others can cite, adapt, critique, and extend when studying AI-mediated storytelling, generative archives, cultural memory, and human-AI design.
The Dissertation Blade
"In generative media systems, description no longer merely represents worlds; it operates systems that generate, revise, stabilize, and contest worlds."

What Must Be True for the Contribution to Count
Validation metrics
Operative description is clearly defined.
Worldtext is distinguishable from imagetext, storyworld, interface, and prompt.
Thick prompting is described as a repeatable research method.
The archive is bounded and structured, not infinite.
The case studies (e.g. Shield of Achilles, WAG) demonstrate the theory.
The artifact/prototype work supports the theoretical claims.
The dissertation committee can easily state the contribution in one sentence.
Future scholars or designers could practically adopt and extend the framework.

Dissertation Defense &amp; Citations
Preparing to defend the contribution architecture
Scholarly justification and literature backing for every node in Watson Hartsoe's dissertation framework.
1. Enters Field (Disciplinary Home)
Digital Media &amp; Media Studies:
Generative AI is a new class of world-forming media. We extend Mitchell’s concept of the
imagetext
(the friction and merging of visual/verbal representations) into the computational
worldtext
.
[Cite: Mitchell, W. J. T. (1994). Picture Theory. University of Chicago Press.]
HCI &amp; Practice-Based Design:
Creative prompting is an agential design practice, not a mechanical routine. We treat it as steering within high-dimensional vector distributions.
[Cite: Zimmerman, Forlizzi, &amp; Evenson (2007). Research through Design in HCI. CHI '07; Gaver, B. (2012). Annotated Portfolios. interactions.]
Narrative &amp; Cybernetic Loops:
Human-AI storytelling is a cybernetic feedback loop governed by statistical viscosity.
[Cite: Aarseth, E. (1997). Cybertext: Perspectives on Ergodic Literature. JHU Press; Wiener, N. (1948). Cybernetics.]
2. Responds to Limit (The Scholarly Gap)
Confronts: Prompting treated as mere technique.
We show prompting is a cultural, narrative, and epistemic practice.
[Cite: Oppenlaender, J. (2024). Prompt Craft as Socio-Technical Art. ACM Comput. Surv.]
Confronts: AI outputs evaluated as isolated content.
We show outputs converge to a default "platform mean" (twelve lighthouses) if not dynamically steered by human agency.
[Cite: Hintze, Proschinger Åström, &amp; Schossau (2026). Autonomous Loops and Convergent Lighthouses.]
Confronts: Authorship debates stuck on human vs. machine.
We shift the binary to distributed agential action. The prompter acts as a cybernetic pilot navigating a statistical medium.
[Cite: Suchman, L. (1987, 2007). Plans and Situated Actions. Cambridge University Press.]
Confronts: AI ethics reduced to bias, hallucination, and consent.
We expand ethics to examine the politics of the generated world itself—what is stabilized, silenced, or defaulted.
[Cite: Bajohr, H. (2024). Platform Realism and the Ethics of Latent Space; Spivak, G. C. (1988). Can the Subaltern Speak?]
Confronts: Media theory lacking vocabulary for executable description.
Modern ekphrasis theory (Spitzer, Heffernan) narrowed ekphrasis to passive artwork description. We recover the ancient production-oriented function.
[Cite: Webb, R. (1999, 2009). Ekphrasis, Imagination and Persuasion in Ancient Rhetorical Theory. Ashgate.]
3. Intervenes Through Method (The Theoretical Wedge)
Operative Description &amp; Ekphrasis:
Description is the control signal that generates visual space in multi-modal manifold environments.
[Cite: Bajohr, H. (2024). Operative Ekphrasis. Digital Rhetoric Journal.]
Thick Prompting:
Anthropological
thick description
adapted to document the agential loops of creative prompting across six distinct context layers.
[Cite: Geertz, C. (1973). The Interpretation of Cultures. Basic Books.]
Worldtext Narrative Systems:
Spatial systems wrapping text-model generation into coherent, persistent environments.
[Cite: Wolf, M. J. P. (2012). Building Imaginary Worlds. Routledge; Aarseth (1997). Cybertext.]
