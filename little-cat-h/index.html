How to read

Swipe left or right to turn pages. BACK and NEXT stay below the artwork and never cover it.

SPREAD opens the original two-page illustration. READ narrates the current page and turns pages automatically. After “Actually—” it continues into the factual postscript.

Arrow keys work on a keyboard. Your place is remembered on this device.
