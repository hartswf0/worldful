Behind the Scenes of the Agentic Multiplane Office & Worktree Pipeline — Persistent agent souls, isolated Git worktrees, cross-model auditing, and the 55-minute handoff

Behind the thirty-four worlds stood a thirty-fifth: a living multiplane studio where named agents walked between slanted light-desks, a brass coffee urn, and a four-column Kanban corkboard.

The Elder: “Every chapter card on the board spawns its own isolated Git worktree and its own seven-plane camera rig.”

In Planning, the agents pinned before-and-after mockups to the wall. In Doing, four parallel worktrees inked forty-five thousand frames on twos. Then the card entered Review and Fix, where a second model audited the drawing.

The Magistrate: “Do not paste a drawing on top of the plate. Throw away the underlay and carve every mountain, cloak, and bird in living cross-hatched ink.”

The Witness: “At minute fifty-five, before the one-hour memory cache expired, each agent wrote its handoff ledger and stepped back to the coffee urn.”

No agent may judge its own work, and no two branches may merge at once. Single-file through the gate, thirty-four worlds became one continuous road.

The Child: “From planning mockup to worktree, from cross-model audit to the master reel: the studio is open, and the projector is running.”
